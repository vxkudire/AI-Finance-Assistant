# Portfolio review concepts
Concentration risk is the risk that a large share of a portfolio sits in one holding, sector or theme. Many investors review whether any single position exceeds a personal comfort threshold.
Asset allocation is the split between stocks, bonds, cash and other assets, and is a major driver of portfolio risk and return.
Rebalancing means restoring target weights after market moves, either on a schedule or when weights drift past a tolerance band. It may have tax consequences in taxable accounts.
The Herfindahl index (HHI) sums squared portfolio weights; lower values indicate a more diversified portfolio.
