# Budgeting and emergency funds
An emergency fund is cash set aside for unexpected costs such as job loss or medical bills. A common rule of thumb is three to six months of essential expenses, held in a safe, liquid account.
A simple budgeting framework is 50/30/20: roughly half of take-home pay for needs, 30% for wants, and 20% for saving and debt repayment. It is a starting point, not a rule.
High-interest debt such as credit card balances is often prioritised before investing because its interest cost can exceed typical investment returns.
