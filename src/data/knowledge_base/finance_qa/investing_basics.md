# Investing basics
Diversification means spreading money across many assets so that one holding's poor performance has a limited effect on the whole portfolio. It reduces company-specific risk but not overall market risk.
An ETF is a fund that holds a basket of securities and trades on an exchange like a stock. Index ETFs track a market index and usually have low fees.
Compound interest means earning returns on earlier returns. Starting early matters because time is the biggest driver of compounding.
Risk and return are related: assets with higher potential returns generally carry higher volatility. Bonds are usually less volatile than stocks but are exposed to interest-rate and inflation risk.
Inflation reduces purchasing power; returns should be judged after inflation ("real return").
