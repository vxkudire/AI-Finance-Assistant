# Capital gains and tax rates
A capital gain arises when an asset is sold for more than its cost basis. Gains on assets held one year or less are short-term and taxed as ordinary income; gains on assets held longer are long-term and generally taxed at lower rates.
A marginal tax rate applies to the last dollar of income; the effective rate is total tax divided by total income. Moving into a higher bracket does not tax all income at the higher rate.
Tax-loss harvesting means selling investments at a loss to offset gains; wash-sale rules can disallow the loss if a substantially identical security is bought within 30 days before or after the sale.
